Form Scanner v6.0

Photograph or upload a paper form — crooked, at an angle, whatever — and get back a straight, cleanly laid out PDF with real fillable fields, following the source form's own structure. Not an OCR text dump: an actual usable digital version, ready to type into.

Built for printed or typed forms with labelled fields and tables — registers, checklists, intake sheets, payment vouchers, and similar. It reconstructs the blank template (not any handwriting already on the page) following the source's own layout — its columns, boxed sections and tables — so the result is a form you can reuse and fill in digitally. Dense handwriting, long prose, and heavily designed layouts aren't what this is built for yet.

Choose a photo or PDF and scan it — the fields, tables and sign-off blocks this tool detects will be listed here first, so you can check they match the original before downloading.
