Form Scanner

Photograph a paper form \u2014 crooked, at an angle, whatever \u2014 or upload a PDF, and get back a clean, genuinely fillable PDF at the same size and orientation as your source by default. Not an OCR text dump: an actual usable digital version of the form, ready to type into.

PDF ready for analysis

Built for printed or typed forms with labelled fields and tables — registers, checklists, vouchers, intake sheets, and similar. It reconstructs the blank template (not any handwriting already on the page) at your source file's own size and orientation unless your note asks for something different. Messier documents — dense handwriting, long prose, heavily designed layouts — aren't what this is built for yet.
