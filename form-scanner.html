Form Scanner

Photograph a paper form — crooked, at an angle, whatever — and get back a straight, cleanly laid out PDF with real fillable fields. Not an OCR text dump: an actual usable digital version of the form, ready to type into.

Built for printed or typed forms with labelled fields and tables — registers, checklists, intake sheets, and similar. It reconstructs the blank template (not any handwriting already on the page), so the result is a form you can reuse and fill in digitally. Messier documents — dense handwriting, long prose, heavily designed layouts — aren't what this is built for yet.
