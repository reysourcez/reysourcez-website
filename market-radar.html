Market Radar v1.7.0

Drop a pin on a candidate spot. Market Analysis ranks which business is the best opportunity there — first-mover, complementary, niche, emerging — and Market Gap shows what would best complete the area. Built from OpenStreetMap, an optional Overture Maps cross-check and Malaysia's open data; never scraped.
