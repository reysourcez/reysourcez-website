How this works

This page encodes your content into a real QR code itself, in your browser — it isn't a wrapper around another site's generator or API. qr-creator-engine.js implements the QR spec directly (Reed-Solomon error correction, mask selection, the works); qr-creator.js turns the result into styled shapes and draws them.

Because everything runs locally, nothing you type — a Wi-Fi password, a phone number, a logo image — is ever sent to a server. There's no Cloudflare Worker behind this tool and there doesn't need to be one.

Styling never touches the actual data: the three corner "eyes" and the general dot pattern can look however you like, but the underlying error-correction math is exactly what the QR standard requires — that's what keeps a styled, logo-bearing code just as scannable as a plain black-and-white one.
