How this works

The guide ratios above are the same table Interactive Costing Analysis, Margin Analysis, and Rental Calculator already use on this site — a scaled-down version of the classic full-service F&B benchmark (roughly 28-35% food cost, 25-35% labor, 20-30% overhead of revenue), leaned out further for a home-based or stall setup and scaled back up for a full store.

A category counts as "Running high" (or "Running low," for Margin) once your entered % sits more than 5 points outside the guide number — close enough that ordinary month-to-month noise doesn't falsely flag something. You can flag or unflag any category by hand regardless of what the numbers say; a gut feeling that something's off is a perfectly good reason to check.

Every cause and every action step is standard F&B cost-control / menu-engineering practice — FIFO, par-level stocking, portion control, yield testing, labor-to-sales scheduling, and the same Star/Plowhorse/Puzzle/Dog framework Margin Analysis's own chart uses. This tool is educational, not a diagnosis of your specific business — for anything material (a lease renewal, a big staffing change), it's worth a second opinion from an accountant or an experienced operator, and we're always happy to be that second opinion too.
