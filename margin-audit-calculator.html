How your true cost is calculated

Every portion's true cost has three parts: its own ingredients, plus a fair share of overhead (fixed overhead and utilities together), plus a fair share of manpower. The two "fair share" pieces are the same for every dish — your total overhead+utilities and total manpower, each divided by how many portions you sell across your whole menu in a month. Only the ingredient cost varies dish to dish.

true cost = ingredient cost + (overhead & utilities ÷ total monthly portions) + (manpower ÷ total monthly portions)
