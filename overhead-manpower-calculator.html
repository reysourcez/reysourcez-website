About the numbers: EPF, SOCSO, and EIS are federal (they apply the same way in Sarawak as anywhere else in Malaysia). SOCSO and EIS now use PERKESO's actual published wage-band tables (Act 4 / Act 800, Category 1, under 60), not a percentage approximation. EPF applies the official Third Schedule rounding rule — the percentage, rounded up to the next ringgit — rather than a bare percentage. This still assumes a Malaysian/PR employee under 60; contribution rates differ for non-citizen staff and staff aged 60+, and PCB/MTD income tax isn't included. Verify with KWSP/PERKESO for anything outside that.
