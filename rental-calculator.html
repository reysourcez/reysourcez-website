Enter your numbers

One tab open at a time — Results, further up the page, updates automatically as you go.

Your equipment

One tab per machine. Operator cost is pulled from your shared manpower pool (set in the next tab) — no wage entry here.

Shared fixed overhead

Rent for your yard/depot, admin, licenses, business insurance — costs that exist regardless of which machine is out on a job. Split equally across every equipment tab.

Add equipment above to see this split per machine.

Shared manpower pool

Your total staff cost — operators, and anyone else — including EPF/SOCSO/EIS, as already worked out correctly in Overhead & Manpower Calculator. Split equally across every machine you've marked "wet hire." A dry-hire machine gets none of it.

Add a wet-hire machine above to see this split per machine.

Revenue-share partners

Anyone you owe a percentage of the rental to. Charged on the price you quote; every price is grossed up so you still net your target after they're paid. Pre-filled with a 5/10/15% split as a starting point.

SST — Sales & Service Tax

As of 1 January 2026, rental/leasing of equipment for industrial or commercial use is subject to service tax at 6% — but only once your rental revenue passes RM1.5 million in a 12-month period. Leave off if you're below that.

Revenue-share and SST together add up to 100% or more of the price — no price recovers your target. Lower a rate below.

How every number here is calculated

1. Ownership cost (fixed, time-based) — shared by dry and wet

monthly depreciation = (purchase price - residual value) / (useful life years * 12) IF financed: monthly installment = principal * r(1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1) [r = monthly rate, n = months; r=0 -> principal/n] avg monthly interest = (monthly installment * n - principal) / n ELSE: avg monthly interest = 0 monthly ownership = monthly depreciation + avg monthly interest + (insurance/year / 12) + (registration/year / 12) ownership / day = monthly ownership / expected rental days this month overhead share / day = (shared overhead / number of equipment tabs) / expected rental days this month

2. Maintenance & wear, and fuel (operating cost, split so dry excludes fuel)

Wear & tear is entered the same way as scheduled maintenance now — a cost and the interval it covers — so you never have to pre-calculate a per-hour rate by hand.

maintenance / hour = scheduled service cost / service interval hours wear & tear / hour = wear & tear cost / wear & tear interval hours maintenance & wear / day (buffered) = (maintenance/hour + wear&tear/hour) * (1 + repair buffer % / 100) * hours per rental day fuel / day = fuel/hour * hours per rental day [wet only -- NOT run through the repair buffer; fuel use doesn't carry repair risk]

3. Operator cost (wet hire only) — from your shared manpower pool

No wage is entered per machine any more. The exact EPF/SOCSO/EIS figure already lives in Overhead & Manpower Calculator; this page only divides that total fairly.

operator / day = (shared manpower pool / number of wet-hire equipment) / expected rental days this month [0 if dry hire]

4. True cost, dry and wet, and the three pricing tiers

dry true cost / day = ownership + maintenance&wear + overhead share + (mobilization fee / short-job days) wet true cost / day = dry true cost / day + fuel + operator DAILY tier -> priced straight from dry / wet true cost / day above MONTHLY tier -> same, but mobilization uses (mobilization fee / monthly-job days) instead of short-job days, then the result is further reduced by your monthly discount % HOURLY tier -> daily price / hours per rental day, then increased by your hourly premium %

5. Margin, gross-up, and member pricing — same shape as your Menu Calculator

target price = true cost / (1 - target margin % / 100) combined share = (SST % + every partner's %) / 100 [must be under 100%] IF "standard hits target margin" mode (default): standard (member) = target price / (1 - combined share) non-member = standard / (1 - member discount % / 100) ELSE: non-member = target price / (1 - combined share) standard = non-member * (1 - member discount % / 100) net revenue = price * (1 - combined share) margin achieved % = (net revenue - true cost) / net revenue * 100 [divided by NET revenue, not the gross price -- this is what makes it match "target margin %" above] By tier: Daily's margin achieved will always equal your target margin % exactly -- that's how the Daily price is built. Hourly and Monthly are priced by adjusting the Daily price (premium / discount), not by re-running this formula on their own true cost -- so their margin achieved is genuinely informative and can land above or below target. See "Margin achieved by tier" in Results.

6. Break-even

fixed monthly cost = ownership (monthly) + shared overhead per machine + operator monthly cost (if wet hire) contribution margin / day = wet daily standard price - (maintenance&wear + fuel + short-job mobilization) / day break-even rental days / month = fixed monthly cost / contribution margin / day

Every rental day beyond break-even in a given month is largely pure profit, since the fixed costs are already covered.

7. Cost-structure guide ratios (rough starting points, not facts)

Mini excavator : 55% equipment / 15% overhead / 12% operator / 18% margin Excavator : 50% equipment / 17% overhead / 13% operator / 20% margin Backhoe loader : 48% equipment / 17% overhead / 15% operator / 20% margin Crane : 45% equipment / 18% overhead / 17% operator / 20% margin Forklift : 55% equipment / 15% overhead / 10% operator / 20% margin Other / general : 50% equipment / 17% overhead / 13% operator / 20% margin

Jargon index
Ownership costThe fixed cost of simply owning the machine — depreciation, financing interest, insurance, registration. Recovered regardless of usage.
Maintenance & wearScheduled servicing plus a wear & tear reserve for parts that wear faster than the whole machine — undercarriage, hoses, teeth, tires. Both entered as a cost and the interval it covers.
Dry hire / Wet hireDry = machine only. Wet = machine plus fuel and operator. Most real market quotes specify which one they mean — always compare like for like.
Hourly / Daily / Monthly tierThree price points for the same machine. Daily is the base calculation; monthly is cheaper per day because mobilization is spread over more days, plus an extra discount; hourly carries a premium since short bookings cost more per hour to service.
Short-job daysHow many days a typical short booking runs — used to spread the mobilization fee for the Daily tier.
Monthly-job daysHow many days a typical monthly booking runs (usually ~26) — used to spread mobilization much more thinly for the Monthly tier.
UtilizationShare of available days a machine is actually rented out — the single biggest lever on cost per day.
Break-even rental daysDays/month needed at your standard price before fixed costs are covered. Days beyond that are largely pure profit.
Margin achieved (by tier)Real profit margin at a given price, after revenue-share/SST, as a % of what you actually keep (not the sticker price). Daily always matches your target exactly, by construction. Hourly and Monthly don't — their premium/discount is layered on afterward, so their real margin can run above or below target.
Revenue-share partnerAnyone owed a % of a job's revenue. Charged on the price you quote, not deducted from your own target.
Gross-upDividing by (1 - rate) instead of multiplying by rate, so a cut comes out of the price without eating your own target.
Shared manpower poolYour total staff cost from Overhead & Manpower Calculator, split across every wet-hire machine here. Precise EPF/SOCSO/EIS math lives entirely in that tool, not duplicated here.
EPF / SOCSO / EISMalaysia's retirement fund, workplace injury/invalidity, and employment insurance schemes.
SST on rental/leasingService tax on equipment rental for industrial/commercial use — 6% as of 1 January 2026, with an exemption under RM1.5 million in rental revenue over 12 months.
