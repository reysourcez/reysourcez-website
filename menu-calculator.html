How it's calculated: Price ÷ (Qty × unit size) gives price per base unit (gram, mL, or each). Yield % is 100% minus Wastage %. True Cost divides price-per-unit by yield, so you see what each usable gram, millilitre, or piece actually costs — after trim, spoilage, or prep loss.
