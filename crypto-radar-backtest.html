Has the score actually worked?

Replays the live Crypto Radar score over past Luno candles without peeking ahead, then counts how often a signal reached your rally target before your stop. It measures the past; it cannot promise the future.

Ready. Fees: Luno MY exchange taker is 0.60% under RM5,000 of 30-day volume (a round trip is 1.2%) and 0.50% from RM5,000 to RM200,000 (1.0%); maker orders are 0.35% / 0.25% each way (source: guide.luno.com, checked 2026-10-04). The box starts at 1.0, so set it for your own tier. The live bid-ask gap is added on top.

Not financial advice. Hit rate = share of signals where price reached the target before the stop. "Median best / worst point" = the typical highest and lowest price inside the window, a data-based guide for profit-take and stop levels. Overlapping signals are not independent, so ranges are widened to compensate. "Hold" = the average result of simply holding the whole window with no stop or target. The rules are fixed in advance, not tuned; because nine are compared at once, a tick mark needs a 99% range, not 95%. "(N mo)" = calendar months the rule fired in: many signals in few months is weaker evidence than it looks. "vs random entry (same days)" compares each rule with random entries on the days that rule could actually have fired: coins are scored from their 80th candle, and rules that need 100 or 200 candles only count once a coin has them. Today's bid-ask gap stands in for the past, so thin coins are probably still flattered.

Big-mover study

Looks at the days followed by a big rally (for example a coin closing +25% within 7 days) and asks what simple rules showed the day before, and how many false alarms came with each catch. Includes new coins with only a few months of history.

Ready. Moves are measured on closing prices from the next open, so a one-trade spike on a thin book does not count.
